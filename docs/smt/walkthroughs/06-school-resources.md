## 1. The starting point (catalogue MIP, 2 weeks, HM)
1. Assess current resources and infrastructure
2. Detail the school's needs
3. Identify resources the community can support
4. List requirements to be procured
5. Share the list with the CRP

## 2. What it already does well
Needs assessment · community support · escalation to the cluster level.
**The gaps:** nothing says **how needs are prioritised, or by whom**, and **nothing is reported back** to parents.

## 3. What was applied, and why

| Method | In plain words | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority vote** (approval ballot) | SMC members and teachers (optionally two student representatives) tick every need they think matters most; the tally goes on chart paper; then the SMC agrees the order | Needs compete, and the SMC is the right body. A visible tally lets every voice count before the discussion. |
| **Knapsack ballot — conditional** | *If* the HM confirms spare money, SMC members choose costed items within that amount | The catalogue MIP mentions procurement but no money. So the tool asks rather than assumes. |
| **Report-back** | A one-page notice: priorities, tally, what's being sought from whom | Families see what was decided and why |

## 4. What was deliberately not done, and why
- **Connected Learning doesn't apply.** There's no student learning activity to improve. Adding "student voice" vocabulary would be decoration.
- **Raising resources is left alone.** Asking the community, gram panchayat, block or donors is already in the MIP and in SMC practice (Prajayatna's SMC Module 2). It isn't participatory budgeting, so the tool doesn't relabel it.
- **No assumed money.** Without a confirmed amount, the budget ballot is skipped.
- **The ballot advises; the SMC decides**, following the module's emphasis on agreement and equal voice for women and men.

## 5. Before → after

| # | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess resources | Same | — |
| 2 | Detail needs | **A numbered shortlist of 5–8, each marked immediate or long-term, with one line of evidence** (including needs of children with disabilities) | changed |
| 3 | — | **SMC priority meeting: approval ballot → tally → SMC agrees the order** | added |
| 4 | Identify community support | Same, **for the agreed priorities**; approached through the SMC or gram panchayat | changed |
| 5 | List procurement needs | Same, for the agreed priorities; **if spare money is confirmed, a knapsack ballot over costed items** | changed |
| 6 | Share with the CRP | Same | — |
| 7 | — | **One-page notice on the board; read at the next PTM** | added |

7 tasks · 2 added · 3 weeks.

## 6. Safety and fairness rules
Women members vote and speak first · picture ballots where needed · procurement stays with the HM/SMC under the usual rules · no sensitive safety details in the notice.

## 7. Teacher card (for the HM)

> # School resources: needs prioritised with the SMC
> **Who runs it:** HM, with the SMC · **Time:** 3 weeks · **Cost:** nil
> **Goal:** Improve the resources available in the school. *Added:* the priority order of needs is agreed by the SMC after a visible vote, and published.
>
> ## Steps
> 1. Assess current resources and infrastructure.
> 2. List 5–8 needs, each marked immediate or long-term, with evidence.
> 3. At an SMC meeting, members and teachers tick the needs that matter most; post the tally; agree the order. *Remember:* women members speak and vote first.
> 4. Find community, panchayat, block or donor support for the agreed priorities.
> 5. List what must be bought. *Only if spare money is confirmed:* SMC members choose items within that amount.
> 6. Share the list with the CRP.
> 7. Post a one-page summary and read it at the next PTM.
>
> ## How you'll know it worked
> - At least 60% of SMC members vote, including at least half the women members
> - The agreed order is in the SMC minutes, and the notice is posted within a week
> - At least one support request (e.g. to the gram panchayat) is sent by week 3
>
> ## Before you start
> - Is any money discretionary (not earmarked)? If not, skip the budget vote in step 5.
>
> ## What changed from the original (and why)
> | Step | Change | Why |
> |---|---|---|
> | 3 | Visible priority vote | So the order of needs reflects the whole committee |
> | 5 | Budget vote only with confirmed money | So nothing is promised that can't be funded |
> | 7 | Public notice | So families see what was decided |
>

## 8. What to watch for
Whether the extra week is acceptable to HMs · whether the tally changes the order compared with what the HM would have chosen (worth recording).
