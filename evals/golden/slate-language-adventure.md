# Golden Example 3 — SLATE Land 1 with house themes (Connected Learning: APPLY as augmentation)

> **Role in the eval set:** Connected Learning applies, but only as an **add-on to an already sound foundational-literacy design**. It tests three rules: keep the objective, no invented evidence, offline by default.
> It also shows the **resource → MIP** step: SLATE is a 24-week programme from the resource library, not a catalogue MIP. The transformer first derives a catalogue-sized baseline MIP from the resource, then transforms that baseline.

## The resource (summary)

- **Who:** student leaders of house teams run English language development for their peers. The guide is written for "Maghizh Muttram" student leaders.
- **Structure:** 24 weeks across four lands, about 6 weeks each:
  1. **Sound Island:** English sounds.
  2. **Word Mountain:** sight words and classroom sight-word charts.
  3. **Sentence River:** reading and explaining sentences; a classroom reading corner.
  4. **Story Forest:** a Learner's Mela showcasing student-made stories.
- **Routine:** two 30-minute peer learning circles a week (Tue/Thu), plus class-wise sessions run by team members. House points are awarded for each.
- **Gate to the next land:** ≥90% of a house's members meet that land's mastery criterion.

The source has **no worksheets or tracking by ticking off pages**; the earlier user-supplied "before" was inaccurate.

## Already present (credit before adding)

Peer-supported (student-led circles), academically oriented (clear mastery gates), shared practices (charts, reading corner), production (Mela stories, but only in Land 4).

## Transformed MIP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | SLATE Land 1: Sound Island, with house interest themes |
| **Target stakeholder** | English teacher (unchanged) |
| **Duration** | 6 weeks (unchanged) |
| **Objective** | *Unchanged:* ≥90% per house know all English sounds. *Added:* every house produces 6 weekly sound-hunt posters on a theme it chose. |

| # | Task | Change | Principle | Student role / artefact | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orient house leaders on SLATE and Land 1 | unchanged | — | — | — |
| 2 | **Each house votes on one interest theme** (e.g. cricket, farming, festivals, the market). Teacher and leaders list 8–10 theme words covering the target sounds. | **added** | Interest-powered | All house members vote | The theme adds words; it never replaces the sound content |
| 3 | Leaders run two 30-minute peer learning circles a week | unchanged | — | — | — |
| 4 | Class-wise sound sessions, **ending each week with a house sound-hunt poster** of theme pictures labelled by starting sound | modified | Production-centred | Poster per week | Drawn or pasted pictures; offline |
| 5 | House points board, **plus a grade-crossing bonus** for every house when the whole grade passes Land 1 | modified | Shared purpose | — | Houses get a reason to help each other, not only compete |
| 6 | Week-6 assessment against the 90% criterion | unchanged | — | — | Mastery gate is untouched |
| 7 | **Poster walk:** houses explain their posters to a younger class or to parents at the next PTM | **added** | Connections across settings | Presenters rotate | Display or spoken only; no photos of children shared outside school without written parental consent (DPDP Act 2023) |

**Size check:** 7 tasks (≤9) · 2 added (≤2) · 6 weeks (≤8) ✔

**Chained follow-on MIPs** (each 6 weeks, same pattern): Land 2 adds a theme-word strip to the sight-word chart. Land 3 adds a house theme mini-book to the reading corner. Land 4 turns the Learner's Mela into a family Story Mela.

### Success indicators
- ≥90% per house know all sounds (non-regression against the source design).
- 6 posters per house; ≥1 poster walk held.
- ≥1 house earns the grade-crossing bonus.

### Rationale (supportable claims only)
Connected Learning describes engagement as stronger when learning connects to learners' interests and is shared with an audience (reference cards: CL-interests, CL-production, CL-audience). SLATE already does the peer part. These additions extend interest and audience without changing the literacy sequence. **The effect on mastery rates is untested**; the non-regression indicator measures it.
