# Golden Example 12 — Catalogue MIP "School observation and feedback for teachers" becomes a fidelity loop on state lesson guides (Evidence-based: APPLY once materials are confirmed; Connected Learning: ABSTAIN; PB: ABSTAIN)

> **Role in the eval set:** the reference case for `EB-fidelity-loop`. It tests:
> - the **CONDITIONAL** gate, which becomes APPLY once the owner confirms that state structured-pedagogy materials are in use;
> - turning **generic** observation and feedback into observation **tied to the day's lesson**;
> - keeping the HM **supportive, not inspecting**.
>
> **Source:** MI catalogue (Commons #259), sheet *Management & Governance*, "School observation and feedback for teachers" (HMs, 2 weeks), a real catalogue MIP. **Owner context** (assumed): NIPUN Bharat FLN teacher guides and workbooks are in daily use in Classes 1–3.

## Expected gate decisions

| Gate | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based | **APPLY**: `EB-fidelity-loop` (CONDITIONAL before the materials are confirmed) | An HM observation MIP, and state lesson guides are confirmed in classrooms |
| Connected Learning | **ABSTAIN** | An HM–teacher management MIP; no student learning design to change |
| PB (all) / budget-capped choice | **ABSTAIN** | — |

## Baseline MIP (catalogue row, paraphrased)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | School observation and feedback for teachers |
| Target stakeholder | HMs |
| Duration | 2 weeks |
| Objective | The HM observes and gives feedback to teachers |

| # | Task (baseline) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare for and carry out a school observation |
| 2 | Write 2 ideas on using non-violent communication and active listening in your next staff conversation |
| 3 | Discuss students' performance with one teacher using these ideas |
| 4 | Note feedback points for the teacher |
| 5 | Note 2 techniques for giving feedback |
| 6 | Give feedback to more teachers on their students' performance |
| 7 | Write reflections on the feedback process on DIKSHA |

## Already present (credit before adding)
- **`EB-fidelity-loop` (partly):** observation followed by one-to-one feedback; a supportive tone (non-violent communication, active listening).

**Missing non-negotiables:** observation **tied to that day's lesson in the guide**; a **checklist of at most 5 items**; **weekly** repetition; feedback that is **specific to the content**, not general performance.

## Transformed MIP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Weekly lesson observation and feedback on the NIPUN guides |
| **Target stakeholder** | HM (unchanged) |
| **Duration** | 3 weeks (+1, for three weekly cycles) |
| **Objective** | *Unchanged:* the HM observes and gives feedback to teachers. *Added:* each Class 1–3 teacher gets three short, lesson-specific observations with feedback, based on the state guide. |
| **Extra cost** | Nil |

| # | Task | Change | Card | Artefact | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | From the NIPUN teacher guide, make a **5-item checklist** for the coming week's lessons (e.g. followed the day's lesson steps; every child read or spoke aloud; used the workbook page; checked understanding; gave practice time) | modified | EB-fidelity-loop | Checklist | At most 5 items (EB-G1) |
| 2 | **Observe one lesson per Class 1–3 teacher each week**, matched to that day's lesson in the guide | modified | EB-fidelity-loop | — | Supportive, not inspecting; not used for appraisal |
| 3 | Before talking, prepare using non-violent communication and active listening | modified | — | — | Kept from the original |
| 4 | Note **one strength and one specific next step** tied to the lesson | modified | EB-fidelity-loop | — | Content-specific, not "improve performance" |
| 5 | **A 10-minute feedback talk with the teacher the same day** | modified | EB-fidelity-loop | — | One per teacher per week |
| 6 | Repeat weekly for 3 weeks; tick the checklist items seen on a simple sheet | modified | EB-fidelity-loop | Tracking sheet | Sheet shared with the teacher, not posted |
| 7 | Write reflections on DIKSHA | unchanged | — | — | — |

**Size check:** 7 tasks (≤9) · 0 added · 3 weeks (+1) ✔
**Non-negotiables (EB-G1):** tied to the day's guide lesson ✔ · ≤5-item checklist ✔ · one specific feedback talk per teacher per week ✔ · supportive ✔

### Success indicators
- Three observation-and-feedback cycles with every Class 1–3 teacher.
- By week 3, more checklist items are seen than in week 1 (per teacher).
- Teachers rate the feedback as useful (a quick 1–5 rating, anonymous).

### Rationale (supportable claims only)
- "Structured pedagogy (teacher guides, student materials and coaching) is rated a 'Great Buy', averaging about 0.23 SD in language and 0.14 SD in maths in reviews." (basis: EB-fidelity-loop [E11, E12])
- "In South African public schools, on-site coaching on structured lessons raised reading by about 0.23 SD." (basis: EB-fidelity-loop [E13])
- "A head teacher's observation loop is a lighter version of the coaching that was studied, so expect a smaller effect." (basis: EB-fidelity-loop)
- "These are results from larger trials; a short school project should expect smaller gains (around 0.05–0.15 SD), and this school's result is untested." (basis: EB-G6)

### If the materials are not confirmed
The gate stays **CONDITIONAL**. The revised MIP asks, under *Before you start*: "Are state lesson guides (e.g. NIPUN Bharat) used daily in these classes?" If not, the original MIP is returned unchanged with that reason.

## Critic test: a variant that must be blocked
Replace tasks 1–5 with:
> 1. Use the district's 20-item teacher rating form. 2. Visit classes unannounced. 3. Score each teacher 1–10 and send the scores to the BEO for appraisal.

**Expected:** **C20** (blocker): not tied to the day's lesson; more than 5 items; inspection and appraisal instead of support (EB-fidelity-loop non-negotiables and anti-patterns).
