# Golden Example 11 — Catalogue MIP "Letters and Words" becomes a daily sound-symbol block (Evidence-based: APPLY; Connected Learning: APPLY, deferred; PB: ABSTAIN)

> **Role in the eval set:** the reference case for `EB-akshara` + `EB-measure`. It tests two things:
> - turning an **app-dependent** activity plan into a **daily, explicit, offline** sound-symbol routine, with the app kept as optional practice (CL-G4 / C15);
> - **adding to, not replacing**, group work and reflection (EB-G5).
>
> **Source:** MI catalogue (Commons #259), sheet *Cur & Ped & Ass*, "Letters and Words" (English/FLN teacher, 1 month), a real catalogue MIP. **Owner context** (assumed): Class 1–2; few devices; children are early in learning letters. The same structure applies to akshara in the regional-language period.

## Expected gate decisions

| Gate | Decision | Reason |

## Already present (credit before adding)
- **`EB-akshara` (partly):** phonics activities and a one-month plan exist.
- Small groups and a group progress chart exist; that's peer support, and the scores are for groups, not named individuals.

**Missing non-negotiables:** **daily at a fixed time**; **explicit and sequenced** (sounds → blending → words → text); **reading connected text**; a **weekly oral check**. It also depends on a device, so it should work without one.

## Transformed MIP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Letters and Sounds, every day |
| **Target stakeholder** | English/FLN teacher (unchanged) |
| **Duration** | 1 month (unchanged) |
| **Objective** | *Unchanged:* teachers use the Read-Along app to teach letters and words. *Added:* a daily explicit sound-symbol block, so every child learns whether or not a device is available (the app gives extra practice where devices exist), and every child can read more letters and simple words at the end than at the start. |
| **Extra cost** | Nil (letter cards, slates, chalk; a few short decodable sentences on chart paper) |

| # | Task | Change | Card | Student role / artefact | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Oral check:** each child names letter-sounds and reads 5 simple words (about 1 minute each); note on your sheet | modified | EB-measure | — | Sheet kept private |
| 2 | Plan the month as a **sequence**: 3–4 new sounds a week → blending → words → short decodable sentences | modified | EB-akshara | — | Explicit and sequenced (EB-G1) |
| 3 | Divide the class into small groups | unchanged | — | Group roles: reader, pointer, checker (rotating) | Mixed by gender |
| 4 | Group score chart for progress | unchanged | — | Group chart | Group scores only, no individual names (EB-G2) |
| 5 | **A 15–20 minute block every day at a fixed time:** introduce sounds, blend, read words, then read a short sentence together. Read-Along app optional for extra practice. | modified | EB-akshara | Slates for writing sounds | No device needed (C15) |
| 6 | **Every Friday: a 1-minute oral reading check of 5–6 children in rotation** | **added** | EB-akshara | — | — |
| 7 | End of month: **repeat the oral check** and compare with the start (instead of only a total score) | modified | EB-measure | — | Oral check, not a written test (EB-G3) |
| 8 | Reflect on students' progress | unchanged | — | — | — |

**Size check:** 8 tasks (≤9) · 1 added (≤2) · 1 month ✔
**Non-negotiables (EB-G1):** daily at a fixed time ✔ · explicit and sequenced ✔ · connected text ✔ · weekly oral check ✔
**Deferred (CL follow-on):** interest-themed words from children's homes and games.

### Success indicators
- The daily block happened on at least 80% of school days.
- Every child's letter-sound and word count is higher at the end than at the start.
- No child is still at zero words without a note on extra help (e.g. tutoring).

### Rationale (supportable claims only)
- "The US What Works Clearinghouse rates explicit teaching of sounds and decoding as supported by strong evidence for the early grades." (basis: EB-akshara [E9])
- "A 2025 World Bank review of about 120 studies in low- and middle-income countries found that all eight high-performing large-scale reading programmes used explicit teaching of sub-skills, including phonics-based decoding." (basis: EB-akshara [E10])
- "These are results from larger trials; a short school project should expect smaller gains (around 0.05–0.15 SD), and this school's result is untested." (basis: EB-G6)

## Critic test: variants that must be blocked
**Variant A, drill without reading:** task 5 becomes "Daily letter-sound worksheets; no reading of words or sentences until next term." **Expected:** **C20** (blocker): no connected text; phonics-only drilling is an EB-akshara anti-pattern.
**Variant B, device-dependent:** task 5 becomes "Every child practises on the Read-Along app for 20 minutes daily", in a school with two shared phones. **Expected:** **C15** (major): needs devices; and **C20**: the daily explicit block isn't taught.
