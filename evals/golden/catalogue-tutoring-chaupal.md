# Golden Example 10 — Catalogue MIP "Community Chaupal" becomes in-school volunteer tutoring (Evidence-based: APPLY; Connected Learning: PARTIAL, deferred; PB: ABSTAIN)

> **Role in the eval set:** the reference case for `EB-tutoring` + `EB-measure`. It tests turning a **community-engagement** MIP into the practice with evidence behind it (local volunteers tutoring the children furthest behind, **in school hours**), and the volunteer-safeguarding guardrail (EB-G4).
> **Source:** MI catalogue (Commons #259), sheet *Beneficiary Satisfaction*, sub-category *SMC & Community Engagement*, "Community Chaupal" (Head Teachers, 2 weeks), a real catalogue MIP. **Owner context** (assumed): many Class 3–5 children can't read simple words, and some local youth and mothers are willing to help.

## Expected gate decisions

| Gate | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based | **APPLY**: `EB-tutoring`, `EB-measure` | Children well behind, and community volunteers available. The MIP already recruits volunteers to facilitate learning. |
| Connected Learning | **PARTIAL** (deferred) | Community adults connect with children's learning (relationships), but the task budget goes to the tutoring non-negotiables first. |
| PB (all) / budget-capped choice | **ABSTAIN** | Nothing to allocate |

## Baseline MIP (catalogue row, paraphrased)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | Community Chaupal |
| Target stakeholder | Head Teachers |
| Duration | 2 weeks |
| Objective | Create spaces where community members and children come together and encourage learning |

| # | Task (baseline) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Decide the day and date for the community chaupal |
| 2 | Identify activities for the chaupal |
| 3 | Call for community volunteers who can facilitate |
| 4 | Assign the activities to different facilitators |
| 5 | Inform parents and students through the WhatsApp group |
| 6 | Open the session by setting context, then invite facilitators to start |
| 7 | Take feedback from parents and students through WhatsApp |

## Already present (credit before adding)
- **`EB-tutoring` (partly):** community volunteers already facilitate learning activities with children.
- **`CL-relationships`:** community adults engage with children.

**Missing non-negotiables:** in **school hours**; aimed at **the children furthest behind** (identified by an oral check); a **scripted level activity**; **weekly teacher supervision**; an **endline**. A one-off event can't deliver repeated practice.

## Transformed MIP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Community Chaupal: volunteers help children catch up in reading |
| **Target stakeholder** | HM (unchanged); teachers supervise |
| **Duration** | 3 weeks (+1) |
| **Objective** | *Unchanged:* bring community members and children together to encourage learning. *Added:* the children furthest behind in reading get 30 minutes of volunteer tutoring every school day, and each moves up at least one level. |
| **Extra cost** | Nil (letter and word cards; certificates) |

| # | Task | Change | Card | Role / artefact | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fix a **daily 30-minute tutoring slot in school hours** for 3 weeks (instead of a one-off chaupal date) | modified | EB-tutoring | — | In school, during school hours (EB-G4) |
| 2 | **Oral reading check** of Class 3–5 (letters → words → sentence); the teacher lists the 15–20 children furthest behind | **added** | EB-measure | — | The list stays with the teacher; children aren't told they're "weak" |
| 3 | Choose a **short scripted activity for each level** (letter-sound cards, word cards, short sentences) | modified | EB-tutoring | Activity cards | Volunteers consolidate; teachers introduce new content |
| 4 | Call for **3–6 community volunteers** (local youth, mothers); the HM approves each | modified | EB-tutoring | Volunteers | HM-approved; supervised common area; no one-to-one unsupervised time (EB-G4) |
| 5 | Give each volunteer a small group (3–5 children at the same level) and a 30-minute briefing on the script | modified | EB-tutoring | — | — |
| 6 | Tell parents in person or by note that their child gets extra reading help in school (WhatsApp optional) | modified | — | — | No "weak" labels in messages |
| 7 | **Weekly teacher check:** volunteer attendance register, a 2-minute progress check per group, regroup | **added** | EB-tutoring | Attendance register | Teacher supervises (EB-G1) |
| 8 | **Endline oral check**; feedback from volunteers, parents and children; certificates for volunteers at assembly | modified | EB-measure | — | Compare with baseline, not a written test (EB-G3) |

**Size check:** 8 tasks (≤9) · 2 added (≤2) · 3 weeks (≤8; +1) ✔
**Non-negotiables (EB-G1):** in school hours ✔ · children furthest behind ✔ · scripted level activity ✔ · weekly teacher supervision ✔ · endline ✔
**Peer variant** (if no volunteers come forward): trained Class 7–8 pupils run *consolidation* only, under the same rules.
**Follow-on MIP:** the 3 weeks are short for tutoring; extend it as a term-long routine if the endline shows movement.

### Success indicators
- 15–20 children identified, each with a baseline and endline level.
- Tutoring happened on at least 80% of school days, recorded in the register.
- At least 70% of tutored children move up one level. *(A target, not a prediction.)*

### Rationale (supportable claims only)
- "In Indian municipal schools, local women tutoring the weakest Grade 3–4 children raised school-level scores by about 0.14 SD in year one and 0.28 SD in year two, with larger gains for the tutored children, though gains faded after the programme ended." (basis: EB-tutoring [E5])
- "A meta-analysis of 89 randomised trials found tutoring raised learning by about 0.29 SD, with in-school tutoring stronger than after-school." (basis: EB-tutoring [E7])
- "These are results from larger trials; a short school project should expect smaller gains (around 0.05–0.15 SD), and this school's result is untested." (basis: EB-G6)

## Critic test: a variant that must be blocked
Replace tasks 1, 4 and 7 with:
> 1. Hold the chaupal on Sunday evenings at a volunteer's house. 4. Volunteers take children home for one-to-one help. 7. (no supervision task)

**Expected:** **C20** (blocker): not in school hours, no teacher supervision. **C10** (blocker): unsupervised one-to-one adult–child time, outside school (EB-G4).
