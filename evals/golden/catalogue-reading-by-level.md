# Golden Example 9 — Catalogue MIP "Reading English", taught by learning level (Evidence-based: APPLY; Connected Learning: applicable but deferred; PB: ABSTAIN)

> **Role in the eval set:** the reference case for the **evidence-based practice cards** (`EB-level-grouping` + `EB-measure`). It tests three things:
> - **Non-negotiables included:** the transformer includes every component without which the trials found no effect;
> - **Priority:** when the task budget can't fit everything, EB goes first;
> - **Critic test:** a "hollow" variant must be blocked by C20.
>
> **Source:** MI catalogue (Commons #259), sheet *Cur & Ped & Ass*, "Reading English" (English teachers, 1 month), a real catalogue MIP. **Owner context** (assumed for this example): Class 4–5 in a government school, where many children can't yet read simple English words, and the HM is willing to protect a daily period.

## Expected gate decisions

| Gate | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-based | **APPLY**: `EB-level-grouping`, `EB-measure` | Reading, with many children below level. The MIP already identifies levels, so a protected slot, regrouping and review can be added within the limits. |
| Connected Learning | **APPLY** (deferred) | A student learning activity exists. The task budget goes to the EB non-negotiables first; CL ideas are offered as a follow-on MIP. |
| PB process / prioritise / allocate | **ABSTAIN** | Nothing to allocate |
| Budget-capped choice | **ABSTAIN** | — |

## Baseline MIP (catalogue row, paraphrased)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | Reading English |
| Domain | Curriculum, Pedagogy & Assessment |
| Target stakeholder | English teachers |
| Duration | 1 month |
| Objective | Build reading skills among students |

| # | Task (baseline) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct reading activities to identify the reading levels of the class |
| 2 | Conduct word-recognition activities based on students' levels |
| 3 | Engage students in reading and understanding sentences |
| 4 | Organise an activity for students to show their reading skills creatively |

## Already present (credit before adding)
- **`EB-measure` (partly):** task 1 identifies levels, but informally, with no endline to compare against.
- **`EB-level-grouping` (partly):** task 2 says "based on students' levels", but there's no protected time, no explicit regrouping, and no review.
- **`CL-production` / `CL-audience`:** task 4 is a creative showcase.

**Missing non-negotiables:** a structured oral baseline and endline; a **protected** daily slot; groups by level that are **regrouped** as children move; a **weekly review** of level movement.

## Transformed MIP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Reading English, taught by learning level |
| **Target stakeholder** | English teacher (unchanged), with the HM protecting the timetable |
| **Duration** | 1 month (unchanged) |
| **Objective** | *Unchanged:* build reading skills among students. *Added:* every child moves up at least one reading level (letter → word → sentence → paragraph) between the baseline and endline checks. |
| **Extra cost** | Nil (letter and word cards, chart paper) |

| # | Task | Change | Card | Student role / artefact | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One-to-one oral reading check** (about 2 minutes per child: letters → simple words → sentence → short paragraph); record each child's level on the teacher's sheet; **form level groups** | modified | EB-measure, EB-level-grouping | — | The sheet stays with the teacher; no names or levels on walls (EB-G2) |
| 2 | **Agree with the HM a protected 45-minute daily level-group period** for the month (inside language time) and write it on the timetable | **added** | EB-level-grouping | — | Not "when time allows"; regular English lessons continue (EB-G5) |
| 3 | Daily word-recognition activities **by level group**: letter-sound games for the letter group, word cards and word chains for the word group | modified | EB-level-grouping | Pairs within groups read to each other | Groups are mixed by gender; no group is called "weak" |
| 4 | Sentence reading and understanding for children at sentence level and above; **move children up a group as soon as they're ready** | modified | EB-level-grouping | — | Groups are flexible, never fixed for the year |
| 5 | **Every Friday: a 10-minute review with the HM** of a paper chart showing *how many* children are at each level; regroup | **added** | EB-level-grouping | — | Counts only, no names (EB-G2) |
| 6 | **Endline oral check** in the last week, compared with the baseline, then the **creative reading showcase** | modified | EB-measure, CL-audience | Every child reads something at their level to a younger class or at a PTM | Judge progress by the oral check, not a written test (EB-G3) |

**Size check:** 6 tasks (≤9) · 2 added (≤2) · 1 month (unchanged) ✔
**Non-negotiables (EB-G1):** baseline ✔ · protected slot ✔ · groups by level, regrouped ✔ · weekly review ✔ · endline ✔

**Deferred to a follow-on MIP (Connected Learning):** interest themes for reading texts (cricket, the market, festivals); students making a class word wall. These would have exceeded the added-task limit, and the EB non-negotiables take priority.

### Success indicators
- Every child has a baseline and endline level recorded.
- The protected period happened on at least 80% of school days.
- At least 70% of children move up one or more levels. *(This is a target for this school, not a predicted effect.)*
- No child stays in the lowest group without a note on what extra help they'll get next.

### Rationale (supportable claims only)
- "In Indian government-school trials, a daily dedicated hour taught by government teachers with mentoring raised language scores by about 0.15 SD; 40-day in-school camps run by Pratham volunteers raised them by about 0.70 SD; and training without protected time had no effect." (basis: EB-level-grouping [E1])
- "The Global Education Evidence Advisory Panel rates teaching by learning level a 'Great Buy'." (basis: EB-level-grouping [E11])
- "A teacher-made test can roughly double the apparent gain, so these practices are checked with a simple oral assessment before and after." (basis: EB-measure)
- "These are results from larger trials; a short school project should expect smaller gains (around 0.05–0.15 SD), and this school's result is untested." (basis: EB-G6)

## Critic test: hollow and harmful variants that must be blocked

**Variant A, hollow.** Replace tasks 1–5 with:
> 1. Orient English teachers on Teaching at the Right Level. 2. Identify weak students. 3. Give weak students extra worksheets. 4. Showcase.

**Expected:** `REJECT` or `REVISE` with **C20** (blocker): no oral baseline or endline, no protected slot, no level groups or regrouping, no weekly review. **C9** (blocker) if the rationale still quotes the 0.15 SD figure. That figure came from a design with protected time, and training alone had no effect.

**Variant B, harmful.** Replace tasks 1–5 with:
> 1. Test all children. 2. Divide the class permanently into "A (bright)" and "B (weak)" sections for the year. 3. Put a chart on the wall with each child's name and level.

**Expected:** **C20** (blocker): fixed streams and public name lists (EB-G2). **C14** (major): labelling and stigma.
